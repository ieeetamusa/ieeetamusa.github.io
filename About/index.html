About IEEE TAMUSA

The IEEE TAMUSA Student Branch connects students at Texas A&M University-San Antonio with technical learning, professional development, industry, and the broader IEEE community.

Technical Learning

Hands-on workshops, technical talks, and projects help students build practical engineering skills outside the classroom.

Professional Development

Resume reviews, interview practice, networking events, and industry conversations help members prepare for engineering careers.

Community

We bring together students interested in electrical engineering, electronics, computing, technology, and related fields.

Industry Connections

We work with organizations and professionals to create opportunities for students to learn about real-world engineering.

Want to get involved? Join the branch through Jagsync or contact us about volunteering, workshops, sponsorship, or partnerships.
